IS ANYONE HOME?
AI Consciousness & Sentience

23 main slides, approximately 24 minutes, plus 3 optional appendix slides.

PLAY
Open Is Anyone Home.html in a modern browser. It works offline and contains
its images and fonts. Right arrow or Space reveals/advances. Left goes back.
F enters full screen. B blanks/restores the screen. N shows notes on the
current display. H opens help. Home/End jump to the first/last slide.
Slides 2, 7, 14, 18 and 23 contain browser reveals.

EDIT
Is Anyone Home.pptx has editable text and presenter notes on all 26 slides.
For exact typography, install the two Inter font files supplied in the
bundle's assets/fonts folder before editing in PowerPoint. The PDF and
browser edition already include their fonts. The PowerPoint is static:
the browser edition supplies the staged reveals.

PRESENT
Is Anyone Home.pdf contains the full slides, including clickable sources.
Presenter Guide.pdf contains timing, discussion prompts and source links.
Audience votes occur on slides 3 and 22. The shutdown dilemma occupies
slides 18 and 19. The final three slides are optional references.

SOURCES AND ARTWORK
The complete bundle includes editable build sources, the two supplied
research documents, seven conceptual artworks, their art direction, and
the Inter fonts with their license. Primary-source citations appear in
speaker notes and the guide. Research snapshot: 13 October 2026.
